Complete the 10-question assessment based on the Psychological Assessment Dataset

🧠 Penilaian Kesehatan Mental
Jawablah 10 pertanyaan berikut dengan jujur untuk mengetahui hasil skrining kesehatan mental Anda.
Pilihlah jawaban yang paling sesuai dengan kondisi Anda dalam 2 minggu terakhir. Pertanyaan berdasarkan Psychological Assessment Dataset (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abdullah895/psychological-assessment-dataset) sebagai penelitian, dengan algoritma Random Forest.
1. How would you describe your mood over the past two weeks? (Mood)
2. On a scale of 1-10, how often have you felt anxious in social situations recently? (Anxious Social Scale)
3. Have you experienced any of the following anxiety triggers in the past month? (Anxiety Triggers)
4. How would you rate the quality of your sleep over the past week? (Sleep Quality)
5. Have you noticed any significant changes in your appetite? (Appetite Change)
6. How often have you felt a lack of interest or pleasure in daily activities? (Lack of Interest)
7. How often do you engage in activities you enjoy or that help you relax? (Enjoyable Activities)
8. Have you had any physical symptoms of anxiety (e.g., heart palpitations, sweating, shortness of breath)? (Physical Anxiety Symptoms)
9. How often do you find it difficult to concentrate on tasks? (Concentration Difficulty)
10. What coping strategies have you used when feeling stressed or anxious? (Coping Strategies)
